## Why

Task bullets are the least legible text on the panel, and a capture analysed pixel by pixel says why: **the screenshotting browser renders text with anti-aliasing off**. A production capture had zero intermediate grey pixels across ~12,000 ink pixels of body text, against 56% in the SVG stars on the same image. Text is rasterised 1-bit; only the drawn shapes get a grey ramp.

Inter's `@fontsource` build is unhinted, so nothing snaps stems to the pixel grid. At 20px/400 a stem lands around 1.5px and rounds inconsistently: **19% of measured stems came out 1px and 62% came out 2px, within the same words**. That unevenness — not blurring — is what reads as damaged text. A 1px stroke at e-ink's grey-not-white background is close to invisible.

The same capture shows the problem is confined to bullets. The 24px/700 name headings measured 3–4px stems and read cleanly, and the small grey elements were judged fine on the wall.

## What Changes

- **Task bullets move from 20px/400 to 22px/400**, with line height 25px → 28px. Measured on the panel, 22px/400 produced the cleanest stems of any setting tested: **80% at 2px and zero at 1px**.
- **Inline `**bold**` emphasis moves from weight 700 to weight 500.** With body at 400, a 500 bold measures a modal 3px stem against the body's 2px — a distinction that survives 1-bit rasterisation. This keeps the emphasis grammar from design.md D12 intact.
- **The task list budget drops from 8 rendered lines to 7**, since 7 × 28px = 196px fits the existing 200px block where 8 would not. `BULLETS_MAX` goes 8 → 7 and `BULLETS_COMFORTABLE` 6 → 5.
- **Font assets**: add `inter-500.woff2` and `inter-500-italic.woff2`. `inter-400-italic.woff2` is already present and becomes the face for `*italic*` at body weight. `inter-700.woff2` is retained for the 24px headings, week labels and grid names; `inter-700-italic.woff2` falls out of use and is removed.
- **NOT changing**: type size or colour anywhere outside the task bullets, the 800×480 canvas, the grid geometry, the render stamp, or the two-week layout.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None. This changes how an existing surface renders, not what the system does.

### Modified Capabilities

- `chart-display`: the legibility requirement gains a rule about stem widths surviving 1-bit rasterisation — the existing rule constrains only the four grey levels and the choice of colour, which cannot govern text whose greys are generated by the rasteriser rather than chosen by the author. The bullet capacity requirement changes from 8 lines to 7, and emphasis is specified as one weight step above body rather than as a fixed 700.

## Impact

- `src/lib/display/Panel.svelte` — `.task-block li` size and line height, `.task-block ul` line budget, `.bold` weight
- `src/lib/display/fonts.css` — `@font-face` for 500 upright and italic; removal of 700 italic
- `src/lib/settings.ts` — `BULLETS_MAX`, `BULLETS_COMFORTABLE`, and the note claiming the typeface survives conversion at every size, which is now measurably false at bullet size
- `static/fonts/` — two files added, one removed, and the provenance table in its `README.md`
- `src/lib/display/Panel.svelte.spec.ts` — assertions on bullet rendering and emphasis weight
- The admin page's over-length warning, which reads `BULLETS_COMFORTABLE`
- **A narrower column**: at 22px a bullet holds roughly 34 characters per line rather than 38, so some bullets that fit on one line today will wrap. Checked against current production content, which still renders in 5 and 6 lines.
- No schema, route, auth, or date-handling change. No dependency change — all weights already ship in the installed `@fontsource/inter`.
